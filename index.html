01 Way in

Every box looks sealed from the outside.

It never is. Before anything gets touched, I enumerate: what is listening, what it is running, what it trusts. This one has three doors.

    02 Rupture

    It holds, it holds — and then it doesn't.

    One seam, the right pressure. The shell never argues for long. This is the moment every black box fears: the first crack of daylight.

    03 Teardown

    Then I take it apart.

    Four disciplines, one habit: understand the mechanism, find where it gives.

      04 The fork

      Same person. Two dimensions.

      What was a black box is now glass — and behind the glass, the path splits. Pick a side. You can always cross over.
